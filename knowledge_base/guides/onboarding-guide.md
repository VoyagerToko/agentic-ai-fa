# Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Orbit Labs!

## Week 1
- Get laptop access, join the chat channel of your team, and meet your team lead.
- Attend the onboarding session run by **Ben Carter** (Platform Team) on Wednesday.
- Read the Secrets Standard and the Logging Standard - they apply to every service.
- Run the Web App locally with Docker.

## Week 2
- Pick a small "good first issue" from your team's board.
- Ship it to production by following the Deploy Guide, with a buddy reviewing.
